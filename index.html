Sheet 01 / Overview

Thermal supremacy at the edge.

MONAD EDGE LLC develops the MONAD-IME (Isothermal Mesh Executive), a deterministic, state-constrained Layer 1.5 thermodynamic control plane operating in Linux kernel space. Designed to prevent silicon thermal throttling and remove the active fan and liquid-cooling bottleneck at the edge.

View evidence
Sheet 02 / Bottleneck

Heat is the constraint the network stack cannot see.

The software that runs the network has no awareness of the physical heat state of the silicon beneath it. Hot nodes keep taking load until frequency scaling forces degradation, dropped packets, or hardware failure. In S.W.A.P. limited platforms, mechanical cooling is too heavy and draws too much power to close the gap.

Sheet 03 / Approach

Steer before it throttles.

MONAD-IME scores path viability in real time by evaluating network and queue friction independently of silicon thermal-boundary proximity.

An asymptotic thermal boundary barrier, paired with hysteresis damping, steers incoming Layer 2 traffic toward cooler compute paths before hardware thermal throttling engages. Across a mesh, the goal is isothermal equilibrium: ΔT → 0.

Formulation, weights, and implementation are proprietary · Patent pending

Sheet 04 / Objectives

Design objectives.

01

Fanless operation

Thermal limits held in software, not by moving air.

02

Waterless heat management

Heat managed at the chip layer, with no cooling water to source or reject.

03

Isothermal mesh

Distributed nodes driven toward isothermal equilibrium (ΔT → 0), maintained in software.

04

Sustained performance

Throttling and frequency collapse preempted under sustained load.
