Sheet 01 / Overview

Thermal supremacy at the edge.

MONAD EDGE LLC develops Zero-Entropy Topology (Z.E.T.), a thermodynamic executive that governs heat at the lowest layer of edge computing hardware. Built for computing where size, weight, and power decide what is possible.

View evidence
Sheet 02 / Problem

Heat is the constraint the network stack cannot see.

The software that runs the network has no awareness of the physical heat state of the silicon beneath it. Hot nodes keep taking load until frequency scaling forces degradation, dropped packets, or hardware failure. In size-, weight-, and power-limited platforms, mechanical cooling is too heavy and draws too much power to close the gap.

Sheet 04 / Objectives

Design objectives.

Zero fans

Thermal limits held in software, not by moving air.

Zero water

Heat managed at the chip layer, with no cooling water to source or reject.

Zero entropy

Deterministic thermal equilibrium maintained entirely in software.

Objectives to be validated on breadboard hardware
